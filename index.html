About Us

Sur Sadhna Music Institute, led by Aditya Sharma, is a vibrant space for music lovers in Jaipur. With over a decade of musical expertise, Aditya brings a blend of traditional and modern training.

Our camp welcomes all age groups to explore the world of music with joy, structure, and creativity. We believe that music is a universal language that connects souls and transcends barriers.

At Sur Sadhna, we focus on nurturing talent in a supportive environment where students can grow at their own pace while developing a deep appreciation for the art of music.

Meet Our Teacher

Aditya Sharma

Founder & Lead Instructor

Aditya Sharma is a young and experienced music teacher, professional singer, and guitarist. With his extensive knowledge and passion for music, he has successfully trained hundreds of students across various age groups.

His teaching philosophy combines technical excellence with creative expression, ensuring each student develops their unique musical voice while building strong foundational skills.
